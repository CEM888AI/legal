# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (Template)

**Last Updated: September 8, 2026**

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("NDA") is entered into by and between
**CEM Unlimited LLC**, a Florida limited liability company ("Company"), and
the undersigned counterparty ("Recipient"), collectively the "Parties,"
effective as of the date of Recipient's signature below.

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship (the
"Purpose") and may disclose proprietary information to each other. This NDA
protects information exchanged for the Purpose.

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by
either Party (the "Discloser") to the other (the "Recipient"), whether oral,
written, electronic, or visual, that is marked confidential or that a
reasonable person would understand to be confidential — including without
limitation: source code, architecture, algorithms, memory/state system
designs, prompts, trade secrets, business plans, customer lists, pricing,
financial data, marketing plans, product roadmaps, and technical
documentation of CEM888.AI and CEM Unlimited LLC.

## 3. Obligations

Recipient agrees to:

- Use Confidential Information **solely** for the Purpose;
- Hold it in **strict confidence** using at least the same degree of care it
  uses for its own confidential information (and no less than reasonable
  care);
- **Not disclose** it to any third party without the Discloser's prior
  written consent, except to Recipient's representatives with a need to know
  who are bound by comparable confidentiality obligations;
- **Not copy, reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble** any software,
  prototypes, or materials embodying Confidential Information;
- **Not use** Confidential Information to develop, market, or improve any
  competing product or service.

## 4. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that Recipient can
demonstrate: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of
Recipient; (b) was rightfully known to Recipient before disclosure without
confidentiality obligations; (c) is rightfully received from a third party
without confidentiality obligations; or (d) is independently developed by
Recipient without use of Confidential Information.

## 5. Required Disclosures

If Recipient is compelled by law, regulation, or court order to disclose
Confidential Information, Recipient will, where lawful, notify the Discloser
promptly and cooperate with efforts to obtain a protective order, disclosing
only the minimum required.

## 6. No License or Ownership

All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. This NDA
grants no license, ownership, or other right in any intellectual property.
Nothing obligates either Party to proceed with the Purpose or to enter any
further agreement.

## 7. Term and Survival

This NDA is effective as of the signature date and continues for **three (3)
years**, except that obligations regarding trade secrets survive in
perpetuity while the information remains a trade secret.

## 8. Return or Destruction

Upon the Discloser's request or termination of the Purpose, Recipient will
promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information (including copies and
derivatives) and certify destruction in writing.

## 9. Governing Law

This NDA is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to
conflict-of-law principles. The Parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction
of the state and federal courts located in Marion County, Florida.

## 10. General

This NDA is the entire agreement regarding the subject matter and may only be
amended in writing. If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder stays in
effect. This NDA may be signed in counterparts.
