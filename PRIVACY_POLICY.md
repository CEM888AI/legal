# CEM888.AI Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: September 8, 2026 · Last Updated: September 8, 2026**

CEM Unlimited LLC ("CEM888.AI," "we," "us," or "our") operates the CEM888.AI
platform, including the website, dashboards, AI agent services, messaging
gateway, and related software (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy
Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the rights
you have. **By using the Service you consent to the practices described
here.**

## 1. Information We Collect

**Account Information.** When you register or purchase, we collect your email
address, chosen display/agent name, and payment information (processed
through our payment processor — we do not store full card numbers).

**Usage Data.** We collect basic usage statistics — feature usage, session
duration, error logs — to operate, secure, and improve the Service.

**Conversation and Agent Data.** Messages you send to and receive from your
agents, and the agent's memory/state, are processed to provide the Service.
Depending on your configuration, this data is stored locally on your device,
on your chosen infrastructure, and/or transmitted through our gateway
servers. **Customer memory, state, and conversations are customer data: they
are not used to train third-party models and are not sold.**

**Local Data.** In local-only configurations, your data remains entirely on
your machine and is never transmitted to our servers.

**Payment Data.** Processed by our payment processor under its own privacy
and security obligations.

## 2. How We Use Information

- To provide, maintain, secure, and improve the Service;
- To process payments and manage subscriptions;
- To communicate about your account, billing, and Service updates;
- To diagnose and fix technical issues;
- To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** use your
conversations or customer data to train third-party models.

## 3. AI Model Providers and Third-Party Processing

Our agents may process your requests through third-party AI model providers
(such as DeepSeek, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or others) to generate
responses. Where we route to such providers, they process data solely to
fulfill your requests, subject to their own policies; you may configure which
providers your agents use at any time, including **bring-your-own-key (BYOK)**
configurations in which requests go directly to the provider under your
account. When you connect third-party platforms (Telegram, Discord, Slack,
etc.) or external APIs at your direction, those services have their own
privacy policies and data practices for which we are not responsible.

## 4. OS-Control and Device Access

When you use the OS-control features of our agents, the agent may access
files, applications, and system resources on your device **as you direct**.
This access is limited to the scope of your explicit instructions, and you
are responsible for what you direct the agent to do.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit
(TLS) and at rest where applicable. Conversation history and account data are
stored on secure servers or on customer-controlled infrastructure depending
on configuration. Local-only configurations store data exclusively on your
device. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we
cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain account and usage data while your account is active and as needed
to provide the Service, comply with law, resolve disputes, and enforce
agreements. You may request deletion of your data at any time; upon verified
request we will delete or anonymize personal data we control, subject to
legal retention obligations. Customer memory/state on customer-controlled
infrastructure is controlled and deletable by the customer.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **Correct** inaccurate data;
- **Delete** your data and account;
- **Export** your conversation history and data;
- **Withdraw consent** or **opt out** of non-essential processing;
- **Object** to processing where applicable;
- **Lodge a complaint** with a supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@cem888.ai. We respond to
verified requests within the timeframes required by law (generally 30–45
days).

**California (CCPA/CPRA).** If you are a California resident: we do not sell
or share your personal information, and we do not use it for cross-context
behavioral advertising. You may request disclosure of the categories and
specific pieces of personal information we have collected, and request
deletion, by contacting privacy@cem888.ai. We will not discriminate against
you for exercising these rights.

**GDPR (EEA/UK).** If you are in the EEA or UK: our legal bases include
performance of contract (providing the Service), legitimate interests
(security and improvement), consent (where requested), and legal obligation.
You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, or
objection as applicable, and you may complain to your local supervisory
authority.

## 8. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies/local storage for authentication and session
management and to remember your dashboard preferences. We do not use
third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking cookies. Where legally
required, we will obtain consent before setting non-essential cookies.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under 18, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has
provided us data, contact privacy@cem888.ai and we will delete it.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy by posting a revised version at cem888.ai
with a new "Last Updated" date. Material changes will be highlighted or
notified by email where practicable. Continued use after the revision date
constitutes acceptance.
