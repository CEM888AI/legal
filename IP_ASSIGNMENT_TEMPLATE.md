# Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement (Template)

**Last Updated: September 8, 2026**

This Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into
by and between **CEM Unlimited LLC**, a Florida limited liability company
("Company"), and the undersigned individual or entity ("Contributor"),
collectively the "Parties," effective as of the date of signature below.

## 1. Background

Contributor has provided, is providing, or will provide services, work
product, code, content, ideas, or other contributions (collectively,
"Contributions") to the Company in connection with the CEM888.AI platform,
agents, software, and related business (the "Business").

## 2. Assignment of Contributions

Contributor hereby irrevocably and unconditionally **assigns, transfers, and
conveys** to the Company, its successors and assigns, in perpetuity and
throughout the universe, all right, title, and interest in and to the
Contributions, including without limitation:

- All copyrights, patents, trademarks, trade secrets, and other intellectual
  property rights therein;
- All rights of priority, registration rights, and the right to sue for past,
  present, and future infringement or misappropriation;
- All claims for damages and remedies with respect to any such infringement,
  whether accrued or not.

This assignment includes all rights in any work made for hire and, to the
extent any Contribution is not a work made for hire, this Agreement
constitutes an express written assignment of all rights to the Company.

## 3. Moral Rights

To the extent permitted by law, Contributor waives any moral rights,
publicity rights, and "droit moral" in the Contributions and agrees not to
assert them against the Company.

## 4. Further Assurances

Contributor agrees to execute, at the Company's request and expense, any
additional documents reasonably required to perfect, record, or enforce the
Company's rights in the Contributions, including assignments for patent
applications and copyright registrations. Contributor appoints the Company as
Contributor's attorney-in-fact to execute such documents if Contributor fails
to do so promptly.

## 5. Representations and Warranties

Contributor represents and warrants that:

- The Contributions are Contributor's original work, or Contributor has full
  right to assign them;
- The Contributions do not infringe or misappropriate any third party's
  rights;
- Contributor has not granted and will not grant any conflicting rights to
  any third party;
- Contributor has disclosed any third-party code, content, or materials
  incorporated into the Contributions.

## 6. Independent Contractor Status

Contributor's relationship with the Company is that of an independent
contributor. Nothing herein creates an employment, agency, partnership, or
joint-venture relationship, and Contributor is not entitled to employee
benefits. Contributor is responsible for all taxes on any compensation paid.

## 7. No Obligation to Use

The Company is under no obligation to use any Contribution, and may decline
or remove Contributions in its sole discretion.

## 8. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, without
regard to conflict-of-law principles. The Parties consent to the exclusive
jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Marion County,
Florida.

## 9. Entire Agreement

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties regarding
Contributions and supersedes all prior discussions and agreements. It may
only be amended in writing signed by both Parties. If any provision is
unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect.
